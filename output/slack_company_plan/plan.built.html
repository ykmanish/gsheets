listen.

The CEO's ChatGPT Pro connects to Slack and works across the channels the CEO can see.

READSearch and summarise messages, threads and channels through the Slack connector.
WATCHEvent-triggered tasks react to new messages in channels where @ChatGPT is added (Plus and Pro, with Work).
ACTReminders, files and joining channels, when the workspace admin allows it.

Triggers for the CEO

WHEN

A payment request of ₹1,00,000 or more lands in #prn-notification

THEN

ChatGPT sums up vendor, project and history in one line and flags it to the CEO.

WHEN

Someone in #site-updates writes about a delay, halt or shortage

THEN

ChatGPT adds it to a running risk list and names who can unblock it.

WHEN

The evening report posts in #uipl-updates

THEN

ChatGPT compares it with the week so far and drafts the CEO's replies.

Good to know

• It works through the CEO's own account, so it sees only what the CEO can see in Slack.

• ChatGPT reads and drafts; approvals and records stay with the UIPL Docs bot.

• Pro's Slack app isn't offered in the EEA, UK or Switzerland; India is fine.

• @ChatGPT for the whole team needs ChatGPT Business or Enterprise; Pro covers the CEO.

Search

Type PRN-1207 or a site name and find the card, the thread, the PDF and who decided.

Clips

Short video or voice clips from a site walk, kept in the thread instead of lost in a chat.
