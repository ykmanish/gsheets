Before it goes live

Five steps
to switch on.

1

Create #uipl-daily

A public channel with everyone in it, for the plain reminders.

2

Create a private HR channel

Add only HR and the app (/invite @UIPL Docs), then paste its ID on Module Control.

3

Bring everyone into Slack

Same email or phone as on the dashboard. 5 accounts still need one added.

4

Create #announcements

Already done. Choose which departments can post.

5

Deploy

The schedule runs on the live server; buttons and approvals work from then on.
