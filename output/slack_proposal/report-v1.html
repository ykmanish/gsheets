UIPL Docs already captures employee daily reports, site manpower (today's actual and tomorrow's plan), and MRN and PRN requests. But the conversation around that work — reminders, approvals, follow-ups — happens on WhatsApp, one phone at a time.

What we propose:

Make Slack the shared room where those updates land, get discussed and get approved. UIPL Docs stays the record behind it; WhatsApp stays only for vendors and site staff who are not on Slack.

Reports are chased one by one

Reminders go out as private messages. Who is still pending is visible only on the admin dashboard, so managers message or call each person.

Manual: chasing late reports
02

Manpower plans travel by forward

Today's actual and tomorrow's planned manpower sit in the DMR. Site leads, procurement and management hear about them through calls and forwarded screenshots.

Manual: compiling & sharing
03

Approvals live in private chats

Each MRN or PRN request reaches approvers separately. Quotations, comments and the decision end up spread across 1:1 threads.

Manual: forwarding context
04

Outcomes need more messages

After a decision, the requester and the concerned people get yet another message each. Accounts and site learn the status second-hand.

Manual: “is it approved?”
05

Failures are silent

94 reminder sends failed this month. Nobody sees a failed WhatsApp send unless they open the reminder log, so the fallback is a phone call.

Manual: noticing gaps
06

Every new alert needs Meta's approval

Business-initiated WhatsApp messages must use pre-approved templates, billed per message. Decisions sit in personal chats with no shared, searchable history.

Manual: searching old chats

05Rollout & decision

A 30-day pilot,
then decide

4 weeks
The reminder, digest and approval logic already exists in UIPL Docs. Slack is a new delivery channel, not a new system.
Week 1Set up

Workspace & read-only posts

  • Slack workspace on the Free plan, five channels
  • Invite office team, PMs, accounts
  • UIPL Docs posts the daily-report digest and manpower updates
Weeks 2–3Act

Approvals & reminders

  • MRN / PRN cards with Approve · Decline · Comment
  • Reminders move to Slack DMs
  • WhatsApp keeps running in parallel as a fallback
Week 4Measure

Review & switch

  • Compare turnaround, late reports and failed reminders
  • Turn off internal WhatsApp reminders
  • Keep WhatsApp for vendors and site staff

Risks, and how we handle them

Not everyone is on SlackSite staff and vendors keep getting WhatsApp messages; Slack is for the office team, PMs, accounts and management.
Two places to lookDuring the pilot WhatsApp is a fallback only; once the numbers hold, internal WhatsApp reminders are switched off.
Free plan keeps 90 daysUIPL Docs keeps the full record either way; move to Pro only if the pilot proves its value.
Decision needed

Approve a 30-day Slack pilot on the Free plan,
starting with Projects and Accounts.
