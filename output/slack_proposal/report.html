01

Reports are chased one by one

Reminders go out as private messages. Only the admin dashboard shows who is pending, so managers message or call each person.

02

Manpower plans travel by forward

Today's actual and tomorrow's planned manpower sit in the DMR; others hear through calls and forwarded screenshots.

03

Approvals live in private chats

Each MRN or PRN request reaches approvers separately. Quotations, comments and the decision spread across 1:1 threads.

04

Outcomes need more messages

After a decision, the requester and the concerned people each get another message. Accounts and site learn the status second-hand.

05

Failures are silent

94 reminder sends failed this month. Nobody sees it unless they open the reminder log, so the fallback is a phone call.

06

Every new alert needs Meta's approval

Business-initiated WhatsApp messages need pre-approved templates, billed per message. History stays in personal chats.

UIPL Docs05 · Rollout & decision
•••

A 30-day pilot,
then decide.

The reminder, digest and approval logic already exists in UIPL Docs. Slack is a new delivery channel, not a new system.

WEEK 1 · SET UP

Workspace & read-only posts

  • Slack Free plan, five channels
  • Invite office team, PMs, accounts
  • UIPL Docs posts the daily-report digest and manpower updates
WEEKS 2–3 · ACT

Approvals & reminders

  • MRN / PRN cards with Approve · Decline · Comment
  • Reminders move to Slack DMs
  • WhatsApp keeps running as a fallback
WEEK 4 · MEASURE

Review & switch

  • Compare turnaround, late reports and failed reminders
  • Turn off internal WhatsApp reminders
  • Keep WhatsApp for vendors and site staff

Cost

Pilot · Slack Free₹0 · 90-day history, 10 integrations

OUR CALL

Slack. PRN approvals are payment decisions.

On Discord they would sit in employees' personal accounts that the company can't manage or take back when someone leaves: the very gap this proposal sets out to close.

WHEN DISCORD FITS

Zero budget after the pilot

Internal staff only, notifications only, and no payment approvals. Since the Slack pilot is free, the price gap only starts after 30 days.
