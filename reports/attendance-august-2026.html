How these figures are built. Present = a record exists for that person and date. A day’s length uses workMinutes where stored; for Dipak Patel on 3 August that field is missing and the length is derived from clock-in to clock-out. Averages for hours cover closed shifts only, so the 19 sessions still open on 10 August are excluded — arrival averages include them, since clock-in is recorded either way. “Typical arrival” on a person’s row is their own mean clock-in; the headline figure is the median across people.
