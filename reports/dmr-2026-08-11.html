Analysis

Today’s DMR records 19 workers across 5 of 9 sites. The sheet itself is largely unfilled: 13 of 243 manpower cells carry a number, equipment, materials and notes are entirely blank, and none of the 10 named staff have an attendance mark. The one genuinely actionable signal is Devsharnam.

Devsharnam planned six and got nobody

It is the only site with planned figures — Electrician 2, Painter 2, Auto Mation 1, Depart Labour 1 — and actual is zero across all four.

This ties directly to yesterday’s employee reports: Milind Chaurasiya logged “Electrical Wires & other materials” as Work Halt at Devsharnam on 10 August. The material blocker has now become idle manpower. Kalhaar carried the same halt on the same material and does have 4 workers today, but no electrician among them.

The sheet is 95% empty

13 of 243 manpower cells filled. Equipment, materials and site notes have zero entries. All 10 staff attendance cells are blank.

The snapshot was taken at 13:44 IST so afternoon actuals may still arrive — but planned figures and staff attendance should already be in by mid-afternoon, and they are not. A DMR with no notes and no material movement cannot answer why anything happened.

Planning is effectively not being used

Planned totals 6 against actual 19. Only Devsharnam has any planned numbers at all.

The company-wide variance of +13 looks like over-delivery but is an artifact: actuals recorded where nobody set a plan. Until planned is filled per site, variance is not a usable metric and the DMR cannot flag a shortfall anywhere except Devsharnam.

Three sites show no activity whatsoever

Escon Bhavnagar, Imperial and Silver white have no planned and no actual figures — not a single filled cell.

Imperial is corroborated by yesterday’s reports: 4 tasks, 1 completed, one of them literally “Awaiting drawing.” For Escon Bhavnagar and Silver white the DMR cannot distinguish “no work today” from “nobody filled the sheet.”

Labour is concentrated in carpentry

Carpenter accounts for 9 of 19 workers (47%), spread thin at 2–3 per site across four sites. Electrician and Auto Mation deployed zero.

Two-to-three carpenters per site is a finishing-stage pattern. With no electrician on any site and automation vendors active in the design reports, electrical work is the trade most at risk of becoming the critical path.

Asteria and Paramdham are properly staffed

Asteria has 6 (Carpenter 3, Painter 3) and Paramdham 5 (Carpenter 2, Marble 2, Plumber 1) — the only sites with more than one trade working in parallel.

Both match the employee reports: Asteria had temple-door and loose-furniture work closing, Paramdham had marble placement and electrical drawings progressing. Manpower and design activity line up on these two.

This snapshot was produced by a dashboard read, not a DMR submission

The document records source: dmr-dashboard-read and was written by Super Admin (AdminUIPL), not by the person filling the DMR.

The project’s own documentation describes dmrDailySnapshots as upserted after successful DMR sheet writes. That is not what created this row. The collection currently holds exactly one document — today’s — so if the mirror only populates when somebody opens the dashboard, days when nobody looks will silently have no snapshot at all.

DMR site names will not join to the other modules

“Farm house”, “S.Gharana”, “Silver white” and “Escon Bhavnagar” here versus “Serenity Meadows Farmhouse”, “Gharana” and “Silver White” in the employee reports.

Any report combining manpower with task progress has to map these by hand, as this one did. A shared site list would remove a whole class of silent mismatch.
